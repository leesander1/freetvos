FreeTVOS on Screen

Every piece of brand art the operating system ships, shown where it appears on the running television and marked by whether the image actually reads it. The firmware and home screens are captures from the rebuilt image in the VM. The splash between them never reaches the VM's display, so it is drawn to its themes' own layout.

The mark, size by size

in the image /usr/share/icons/hicolor/<size>/apps/freetvos.png

Found by name through os-release's LOGO=freetvos. Sizes up to 128 are shown at their real pixel size; the smallest are also enlarged four times with hard edges, which is where the separate small drawing earns its place.

{{LADDER}}

At 16px the full mark's stand and foot smear into one shape and the play symbol is a speck, so 24px and below come from brand/logo/freetvos-small.svg: the tile runs to the edge and every part is at least two pixels.
